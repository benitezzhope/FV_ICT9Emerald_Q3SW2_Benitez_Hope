Recommended Valentine's Flowers 💘


  • Roses
    • The most popular choice, specifically red for romance, pink for affection, and white for purity.


  • Gerberas
    • Three gerberas symbolize "I love you," where the gesture silently conveys deep romantic feelings, especially when words fail.


  • Carnations
    • Said to symbolise devotion, love and fascination, they're the perfect swap if you fancy a change from that bouquet of roses for your partner.


  • Peonies
    • Peonies are rich in symbolism, often representing romance, prosperity, and good fortune. Often associated with love and marriage, they're a popular choice for weddings and romantic occasions.
